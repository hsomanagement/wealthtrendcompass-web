The Founder's Framework (Developed & Refined Since 2008)

Wealth Trend Compass was born out of real-world necessity following the Great Financial Crisis of 2008. Watching years of hard-earned family savings suffer severe drawdowns sparked a systematic research effort into institutional regime indicators that measure elevated macroeconomic stress before deep market contractions unfold.

By combining macroeconomic credit spread ratios, options market volatility term structure, and sector breadth telemetry, this quantitative observation framework was built to monitor major market cycles objectively. Today, it brings calm, institutional-grade telemetry directly to self-directed investors worldwide.
